# Leaf & Lantern — Image Generation Prompts

Scene assets for the site live in `public/images/`. Use these prompts to regenerate any image
in ChatGPT, then save to the exact path listed.

> **Tip:** ChatGPT exports PNG. Save with the exact filename below (PNG is fine as-is). If you
> want smaller files, convert with:
> `sips -s format jpeg -s formatOptions 78 -Z 1800 input.png --out public/images/<name>.png`
> (keeping the `.png` filename works — browsers read the format from the file contents).

## Shared style guidance (prepend to every prompt)

> Photorealistic editorial photograph for a Michigan seasonal gathering place called Leaf &
> Lantern. Modern heritage aesthetic: dark timber, cream-painted signage, stone bases, warm
> shielded lantern lighting, natural materials. Restrained and premium but approachable —
> never an amusement park, festival crowd, faux-rustic theme park, or luxury wedding estate.
> Guests look natural and unposed: talking, browsing, choosing, learning, or making — never
> lined up for the camera. Landscape orientation, 3:2 aspect ratio.
>
> **Typography in images:** Do not match the website’s Fraunces wordmark. Keep the same
> painted-sign / concept aesthetic already used in the existing Leaf & Lantern reference
> images. When signage is readable, use "LEAF & LANTERN" with "MORE THAN GREAT CIDER AND
> DONUTS" beneath — never "A Seasonal Gathering Place."

**Prompt:**

> A bright spring morning at Leaf & Lantern in Michigan. Eight to twelve children ages six to
> ten with two adult educators at raised garden beds and outdoor teaching tables beside a
> lodge-like timber building. Children are testing soil with trowels, examining seedlings with
> magnifiers, and comparing seeds in small trays. Fresh green foliage, early flowers, soft
> morning light. Organized materials, attentive atmosphere, real hands-on learning. No autumn
> decorations, no pumpkins, no crowds, no playground equipment.

**Prompt:**

> The order-ahead pickup counter at Leaf & Lantern on a busy but comfortable autumn morning. A
